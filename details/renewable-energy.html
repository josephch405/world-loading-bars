Key Insights

Solar Surge

Solar PV has grown at over 25% annually, becoming the fastest-growing power source in human history and driving most new capacity additions.

Overtaking Coal

In 2025, total global electricity generation from renewables officially surpassed coal for the first time in modern industrial history.

Grid Storage Essential

Battery energy storage deployments expanded tenfold between 2020 and 2025 to balance variable solar and wind generation on electrical grids.
