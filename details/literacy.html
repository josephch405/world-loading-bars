Key Insights

Youth Literacy Advantage

Youth literacy (ages 15–24) has reached over 93% globally. As younger, educated generations age into adulthood, global adult literacy is projected to approach 95% by 2040.

Gender Gap Progress

The literacy gender gap has narrowed substantially over the past three decades. While women still constitute ~64% of illiterate adults globally, youth gender parity in literacy has been achieved in most world regions.

Regional Disparities

Developed countries and East Asia report 99%+ literacy. Sub-Saharan Africa and Southern Asia face the largest remaining challenges, though adult literacy in both regions has grown by 20+ percentage points since 1990.

Digital Literacy Expansion

Modern literacy definitions increasingly encompass digital literacy—the ability to navigate, evaluate, and produce information using digital devices and internet platforms.
