Eradication Timeline

1796
First Vaccine Created

Edward Jenner developed the cowpox vaccine, creating the world's first successful vaccine.

1967
WHO Intensified Campaign

Global intensified eradication initiative launched using bifurcated needles and ring vaccination.

1977
Last Naturally Occurring Case

Ali Maow Maalin in Somalia was the last person infected with naturally occurring Variola minor.

1980
100% Global Eradication Declared

33rd World Health Assembly officially declared the world free of smallpox.

Key Insights

Lives Saved

Smallpox killed an estimated 300–500 million people in the 20th century alone. Its eradication saves roughly 5 million lives every single year.

Economic ROI

The entire global eradication effort cost ~$300 million USD, saving the world over $1 billion annually in treatment and containment costs forever.

Ring Vaccination Method

Instead of mass universal vaccination, epidemiologists isolated infected individuals and vaccinated everyone in their immediate contact perimeter.
