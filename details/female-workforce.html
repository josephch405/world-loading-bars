Key Insights

Regional Variation

Nordic countries reach 85%+ parity through equal parental leave and universal childcare support. Middle East and North Africa average ~28% parity.

Economic Impact

McKinsey estimates achieving full workforce gender parity could add $12 trillion to global GDP by boosting family living standards and national productivity.

Care Infrastructure Gap

Unpaid caregiving (childcare, eldercare) falls disproportionately on women, consuming 3x more hours than men globally. Expanding care services directly elevates workforce parity.

Digital Opportunities

Remote work, flexible digital freelancing, and targeted STEM initiatives are accelerating workforce parity in developing and developed markets alike.
