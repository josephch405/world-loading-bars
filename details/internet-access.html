Key Insights

Rapid Expansion

Internet access has quadrupled since 2005, driven by mobile technology and falling costs of smartphones and mobile data plans.

Digital Divide

While high-income nations exceed 93% connectivity, low-income nations remain around 23-27%, representing a crucial gap for global equity.

Future Goals

UN targets universal internet access by 2030. Satellite internet mega-constellations and 5G expansion promise to bridge remote coverage gaps.
