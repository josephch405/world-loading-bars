The 3 Pillars of Commercial Fusion

1. Ignition (Achieved)

Producing more thermal energy out of the fusion reaction than the laser/magnetic energy input.

2. Engineering Gain (Q_engineering > 1)

Generating net electricity accounting for total wall-plug efficiency of power systems.

3. Grid Integration

Building scalable pilot plants producing electricity continuously under 5¢/kWh.
