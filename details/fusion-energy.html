Key Fusion Milestones

1997
JET Tokamak Q = 0.67

Record 16.1 MW of fusion power achieved at Culham Centre for Fusion Energy in the UK.

2022
Net Energy Gain (Q > 1)

National Ignition Facility (NIF) achieved net fusion energy gain (3.15 MJ output vs 2.05 MJ laser energy input).

2025
NIF Target Gain Record (Q_t = 4.13)

On April 7, 2025, LLNL's National Ignition Facility set an official record delivering 8.6 MJ fusion yield from 2.08 MJ laser input, using tungsten-doped diamond capsules.

2026
SPARC Tokamak & Commercial PPAs

Commonwealth Fusion Systems reaches ~75% construction completion on the SPARC HTS tokamak targeting net-energy testing, while Helion advances its 2028 Microsoft power agreement.

The 3 Pillars of Commercial Fusion

1. Ignition (Achieved)

Producing more thermal energy out of the fusion reaction than the laser/magnetic energy input.

2. Engineering Gain (Q_engineering > 1)

Generating net electricity accounting for total wall-plug efficiency of power systems.

3. Grid Integration

Building scalable pilot plants producing electricity continuously under 5¢/kWh.
