Key Insights

Exponential Growth

Global electric car sales share has grown from ~4% in 2020 to 25% of all new car sales, exceeding 20 million units sold annually.

Regional Leaders

China leads with ~55% of new sales being electric/PHEV, Europe averages ~28%, and Norway leads globally with over 90% EV market share.

Future Outlook

IEA Global EV Outlook projections show global EV market share reaching ~30% (~23 million units) in 2026 and over 40% by 2030.
