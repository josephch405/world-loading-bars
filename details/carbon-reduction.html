Key Insights

Divergent Global Paths

While developed nations cut GHG emissions by 18.5% since 1990, total global emissions grew driven by industrialization in emerging markets, making global decoupling essential.

Regional Leaders

The EU has cut greenhouse emissions by over 30% since 1990 and the UK by over 45%, showing that economic growth can be decoupled from carbon emissions.

LULUCF Impact

When including Land Use, Land-Use Change, and Forestry (LULUCF), developed country emissions reductions reach 22.3% below 1990 levels.
