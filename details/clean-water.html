Key Insights

Basic vs. Safely Managed

While 91.4% of humanity has access to an improved water source within a 30-minute round trip (basic access), 74.0% has "safely managed" drinking water directly piped to premises free of contamination.

Urban vs. Rural Disparities

Urban water coverage exceeds 97% for basic access, whereas rural areas—particularly in Sub-Saharan Africa and Oceania—lag behind at under 82% basic coverage.

Health & Economic Impact

Universal access to clean water reduces waterborne diseases (cholera, typhoid, dysentery) and saves millions of hours daily—predominantly spent by women and girls fetching water.

Climate & Water Scarcity

Climate change, droughts, and groundwater depletion pose growing threats to water security, requiring investment in desalination, water recycling, and infrastructure repair.
