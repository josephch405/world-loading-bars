Key Insights

Rapid Growth

Secondary education completion has doubled globally since 1980, from ~30% to 61%. This expansion reflects urbanization, economic development, and policy prioritization of secondary education.

Economic Necessity

In modern economies, upper secondary completion is essential for skilled employment. OECD countries average 85%+ completion, while sub-Saharan Africa reaches ~28%, reflecting regional gaps.

Persistent Barriers

Poverty, early labor market entry, lack of secondary school infrastructure in rural areas, and conflict zones remain the principal obstacles to universal completion.

Skills Gap Challenges

Focus is increasingly turning from enrollment numbers to educational quality, technical and vocational education (TVET), and digital skills necessary for the modern economy.
