Key Insights

Primary Gateway

For billions of people in developing countries, mobile phones serve as the primary or sole device for accessing the internet, financial services, and healthcare.

Subscription Density

Global mobile subscriptions reached 112 per 100 inhabitants in 2024, reflecting widespread multi-SIM usage and connected IoT hardware.

Infrastructure Shift

5G networks now cover over 50% of the global population, enabling high-speed mobile broadband even where fixed fiber broadband is absent.
