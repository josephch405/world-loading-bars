Key Insights

Biodiversity Hotspots

Protected areas safeguard habitats for 80% of terrestrial species. Priority zones include the Amazon, Congo Basin, and Southeast Asian rainforests.

The 30x30 Target

The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework targets 30% terrestrial protection by 2030, requiring an expansion of 12.4 percentage points globally.

Indigenous Stewardship

Territories managed by Indigenous peoples encompass 40% of protected lands worldwide and exhibit higher biodiversity health than state-only reserves.
