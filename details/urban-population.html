Key Insights

Economic Engine of Growth

Cities generate over 80% of global GDP. Urban density accelerates innovation, specialization, trade, and access to education and modern health facilities.

Megacity Expansion

In 1950, only New York and Tokyo were megacities (10M+ inhabitants). Today, there are 33+ megacities, with the largest growth occurring in Asia and Africa.

Environmental Footprint

While cities consume ~78% of global energy and produce over 60% of greenhouse emissions, urban density can also enable efficient public transport and shared energy infrastructure.

Infrastructure Demands

Rapid urban migration creates critical demands for affordable housing, clean water networks, sanitation, electricity, transit systems, and climate disaster resilience.
