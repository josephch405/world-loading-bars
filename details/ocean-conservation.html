Key Insights

10% Milestone Achieved

Total designated marine protected and conserved areas reached 10.0% of world oceans, marking significant formal designation progress toward the 30x30 goal.

High Seas Treaty (BBNJ)

The UN High Seas Treaty enables marine protected area creation in international waters (64% of oceans), safeguarding open-ocean migratory pathways.

Protection Quality Gap

While 10.0% is officially designated, MPAtlas estimates ~3.3% is fully or highly protected from industrial fishing and extraction, emphasizing enforcement needs.
