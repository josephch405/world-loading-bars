Key Insights

DTP3 as Health Benchmark

Diphtheria-Tetanus-Pertussis (DTP3) coverage is the global gold standard indicator for evaluating the reach and efficiency of routine health systems.

Pandemic Recovery

Childhood vaccination suffered the largest backslide in 30 years during the COVID-19 pandemic (dropping to 81% in 2021). Coverage has rebounded back to 85% globally.

Zero-Dose Children Challenge

Despite 85% coverage, approximately 14.3 million children worldwide are "zero-dose" (received no vaccines at all), concentrated in remote and conflict-affected areas.

New Vaccine Introductions

Beyond DTP, global rollouts of HPV, pneumococcal, rotavirus, and new malaria vaccines (RTS,S and R21) are rapidly improving child survival worldwide.
