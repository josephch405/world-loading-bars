Key Insights

Historic Transformation

The share of humanity living outside extreme poverty rose from ~62% in 1990 to over 91.5% today. This represents one of the most rapid reductions in human deprivation in history.

Regional Shift

In 1990, East Asia accounted for the majority of the world's extreme poor. Today, Sub-Saharan Africa accounts for roughly two-thirds of the remaining population in extreme poverty.

Higher Poverty Lines

While extreme poverty ($2.15/day) has fallen below 9%, higher poverty thresholds ($3.65/day and $6.85/day) reveal that nearly 47% of the global population still struggles to meet basic needs.

Post-Pandemic Challenges

The COVID-19 pandemic and global inflation shocks temporarily stalled progress. Re-accelerating progress requires target investment in agriculture, climate resilience, and social protection safety nets.
