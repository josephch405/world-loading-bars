Key Insights

Sub-Saharan Concentration

Over 80% of the 655 million people currently lacking electricity reside in Sub-Saharan Africa, where rapid population growth challenges grid development.

Distributed Solar

Pay-as-you-go solar systems and community microgrids are replacing expensive centralized grid extensions for remote communities.

SDG 7 Horizon

UN Sustainable Development Goal 7 targets universal access by 2030, requiring a tripling of current annual electrification rates.
