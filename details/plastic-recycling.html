Key Insights

Production Outpaces Recovery

With over 430 million tonnes of plastic produced annually, total waste volume expands faster than municipal mechanical sorting infrastructure.

Regional Disparities

EU packaging recycling reaches ~32-38%, while low-income regions hover under 2% due to informal waste sectors and missing collection systems.

UN Plastics Treaty

The landmark legally binding Global Plastics Treaty establishes global packaging standards, virgin plastic phaseouts, and recycling mandates.
